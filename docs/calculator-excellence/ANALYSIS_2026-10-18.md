# FinPath Multi-Agent Calculator Analysis & Ranked Fix Backlog (2026-10-18)

## 1. Executive Summary & Multi-Agent Workflow Provenance

This document records the comprehensive multi-agent calculator analysis across all **84 public calculator routes** (82 registry calculators in `src/lib/seoCalculators.ts`, the dedicated `/calculators/fire` planner, and the `/calculators` hub) across all 7 toolkits ("studios").

### 4-Stage Verification Architecture
1. **Stage 1 — Deterministic Route Inventory (No Model):** A read-only Vitest capture script evaluated every route at its default inputs, scenario perturbations (`Conservative` / `Base` / `Optimistic` or `Case A` / `Your inputs` / `Case B`), sensitivity drivers (`buildCalculatorInputImpacts`), schedule tables at both `default` term and `default + 0.5` fractional term, chart series, and rendered DOM copy.
2. **Stage 2 — 7 Parallel Toolkit Auditors (`toolkit-auditor`):** Seven domain-specialized subagents audited their respective studios (`Loan & Home`, `Growth & Goal`, `Retirement Income`, `Income & Tax`, `Debt Payoff`, `Return Analysis`, `Cashflow & Balance Sheet`) against independent Python `Decimal` oracles and primary US/India statutory sources (IRS, CFPB, SSA, RBI, India Post PPF Scheme 2019, EPF Scheme 1952, PFRDA, Income Tax Department, Payment of Gratuity Act 1972).
3. **Stage 3 — 7 Parallel Adversarial Verifiers (`adversarial-verifier`):** Seven independent adversarial subagents attempted to disprove every finding by re-reading the TypeScript engine/schedule/test files, checking prior checkpoint contracts (`C15`–`C29` / `B47`–`B67`), and re-running independent `Decimal` and `npx tsx` probes.
   - **Total Initial Findings Audited:** 68
   - **Confirmed Intact (`CONFIRMED`):** 41
   - **Survived with Narrowed Scope/Severity (`NARROWED`):** 27 (false or already-contracted sub-claims were removed; see each finding's verifier verdict)
   - **Post-Verification Severity Distribution:** **4 `P0`**, **29 `P1`**, **31 `P2`**, **4 `P3`**
4. **Stage 4 — Ranked Bounded Contracts:** All surviving findings are grouped below into bounded, independently verifiable implementation checkpoints (`B68`–`B76`), interleaved with the staged full-site visual redesign checkpoints.
