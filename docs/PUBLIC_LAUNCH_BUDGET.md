## Smallest owner inputs and safe release order

1. Choose an available non-premium domain and exact app hostname; specify DNS provider. Owner buys it and explicitly authorizes any DNS edits. Clerk's [production setup](https://clerk.com/blog/how-to-take-clerk-to-production) and [environment documentation](https://clerk.com/docs/guides/development/managing-environments) describe the custom-domain production flow.
2. Complete the owned Clerk production instance and provider/domain validation. Enter live values only through the repository's secure setup path, never chat/source. See [production-auth runbook](PRODUCTION_AUTH_RUNBOOK.md). Exact required frontend names: `VITE_CLERK_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`, `FINPATH_PRODUCTION_ORIGIN`; Pages secret names: `CLERK_PUBLISHABLE_KEY`, `CLERK_SECRET_KEY`, `CLERK_AUTHORIZED_PARTIES`.
3. Astra verifies 6/6 preflight and binding/origin isolation, records a production restore bookmark, then follows existing OA-3 authorization for migrations and the separately gated release. Hosted sign-up/sign-in/sign-out/session/save/export/deletion and tenancy must pass before launch-ready is claimed.
4. For a future paid offer: business country/entity, billing/refund/support contacts, approved prices and tax treatment. These are not blockers to a free public calculator release.
5. For B51 validation: recruit 10 usability participants (5 US/5 India) and 20 real plan creators, with consent; no personal financial records need to be shared with agents. Recruitment is owner action; this turn sends nobody a message.

Recheck vendor prices and plan limits before paying or moving production. Free infrastructure still requires operational attention, data recovery and security review. The existing preflight is preserved; no purchases, DNS changes, paid campaigns or production writes were performed in this audit.
