# B63 — Exact-term schedules for return, inflation, Roth/traditional and EPF tables

C26. Same owner continuation ("finish the work you can do without me unblocking it"). Follows the B62 residual audit. Baseline: main after C25 (PR #148).

**Audit (2026-10-03, read-only, every route with a `years` input at its default and default + 0.5):** whole-year terms reconcile; at a part-year term these routes' tables contradict the headline because their schedule builders use `ceil(years)` while the engines use the exact term:

| Route(s) | Engine (inspected) | Baseline headline vs table at +0.5 year |
|---|---|---|
| `investment-return`, `cagr` | `(final/initial)^(1/years) − 1` | 5.5 yrs: headline 11.28%; table ends at "6" and its Annualized-return column shows 10.29% |
| `inflation` | `principal × (1 + rate)^years` | 10.5 yrs: $15,095.59 vs final row $15,394.54 |
| `roth-vs-traditional-ira` | `contribution × (1 − current tax) × (1 + rate)^years` | 25.5 yrs: $30,653.42 vs $31,708.15 |
| `epf` | monthly, `termMonths(years)` | 10.5 yrs: ₹47,15,707.76 vs ₹50,53,929.41 |

Independent Python Decimal oracles: CAGR `1.8^(1/5.5) − 1 = 0.1127899192821328`; inflation `10000 × 1.04^10.5 = 15095.588987365667`; Roth `7000 × 0.78 × 1.07^25.5 = 30653.42267604345`; EPF 126 end-of-month deposits of 24,000 at 8%/12 `= 4715707.764417067`.

**Not in scope (domain decisions, recorded as residuals):** PPF — yearly deposits make a fractional PPF term a product question; gratuity — the Payment of Gratuity Act counts a part year over six months as a year, so the engine's raw fractional multiplier is a legal-rule question needing its own sourced contract. Other routes flagged at whole years have non-balance headlines (monthly target, comparisons) and are not contradictions.
