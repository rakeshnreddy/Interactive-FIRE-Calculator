## Outcome and acceptance

- **R1 (Spacing & layout):** `src/home.css` and `src/shell.css` use only 4px-grid spacing (`0, 2, 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40, 48, 56, 64, 72px` plus `clamp()` expressions whose bounds are on that grid). Sections and order are unchanged.
- **R2 (Colour & surfaces):** No non-repeating `linear-gradient(`/`radial-gradient(`, no SVG `<linearGradient>`/`<radialGradient>`, and no non-`none` `backdrop-filter` in `src/home.css`, `src/shell.css`, or `src/HeroFireExample.tsx`. The topbar is an opaque `--color-nav-solid` surface with one 1px hairline (removing translucent `--color-nav-surface` glass blur). The hero example is a raised surface with a 1px hairline, a `12px` outer radius matching Stage A/B panels, no 3px accent top stripe, and a quiet neutral rest shadow (none in dark). `--shadow-overlay` and `--shadow-rest` in `src/vivid-theme.css` use neutral black alphas (no tinted blue wash; `--shadow-rest: none` in dark).
- **R3 (Icons):** Every Lucide UI icon in `src/LandingPage.tsx` and `src/HeroFireExample.tsx` renders at `16` or `18` with `strokeWidth={1.5}`; the brand mark stays a single flat glyph.
- **R4 (Copy & type):** H1 `Clear answers to your money questions` and H2s `Start with a question, leave with a clearer plan`, `Honest math, in your currency` and `Start without an account` drop trailing periods. The finder submit becomes a secondary `Search` button so the hero has one primary action, one secondary action and the search field. Weights in both files are limited to `400`, `500`, `600`; display letter-spacing ≤ `-0.03em`; small text has no negative tracking.
- **R5 (Details & motion):** Nested radii add up within the unified Stage A/B/C `<= 12px` scale (`12px` containers with `4px` padding hold `8px` items; `8px` controls; `4px` inner input focus); footer group headings use the footer muted role; hero SVG uses `strokeWidth="2"`, a quiet flat solid-tint area fill, and a horizontal hairline mid-rule; transitions use `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` at `120–180ms`; no `!important` outside `@layer accessibility` or `@media print`; reduced-motion, reduced-transparency, forced-colors and print fallbacks remain.
- **R6 (Verification & release):** Red-phase `src/UnslopStageC.test.tsx` first; update existing copy assertions in `src/landingPage.test.tsx`, `src/LandingFinder.test.tsx` and `scripts/smoke/b31-reader.mjs` to the new strings only. Pass `./scripts/test_all.sh`, an independent adversarial review, exact-head CI, candidate and shared-beta deploys, `smoke_public_calculators.mjs` (84 routes) and a new shared-runner scenario `c40-unslop-stage-c` (homepage anonymous at `1440/768/390/320` × light/dark with the Stage B computed-style audit, finder journey, hero fixture `$965,931` and keyboard disclosure; shell audit on `/`, `/calculators` and `/calculators/mortgage`). Then merge to `main`.
