Add a prominent sample badge on initial registry examples, Your inputs after explicit edits, and deterministic Reset to example. A loaded historical result follows the same previous-result contract during pending edits; its stored snapshot stays immutable.

Sample/current/stale states are distinct and accessible. Reset and draft restoration reproduce their documented state; typed invalid data cannot be saved as a current estimate.
