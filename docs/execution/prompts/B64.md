# B64 — PPF: whole financial years and the yearly deposit limit

C27. Owner said "continue" after C26; follows the B63 residual. Baseline main `6413640cc90019c25fc77bbf2592a979747ff98a`.

**Primary source, read 2026-10-04:** [Public Provident Fund Scheme, 2019 (G.S.R. 915(E), 12 December 2019), India Post](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/documents/offerings/schemesandservices/posb/PublicProvidentFundScheme2019English.pdf) — text extracted locally from the official PDF. Para 2(1)(f): "year" means the financial year. Para 4(1): a deposit of not less than ₹500 and not more than ₹1,50,000 may be made in a year. Para 5(2): in one lump sum or instalments. Para 7: interest for a calendar month on the lowest balance between the close of the fifth day and the end of the month, credited at the end of each year. Para 11: closure after fifteen years from the end of the year of opening. Para 12: extension for further blocks of five years. The current notified rate is not taken from this document; the rate stays an entered assumption.

**Source convention (inspected):** `ppf` computes an annuity-due `annual × ((1+r)^years − 1)/r × (1+r)` with contributions `annual × years` — a full yearly deposit made by 5 April earning the full year's interest, credited yearly, consistent with para 7. A fractional term is accepted and produces impossible results.

**Reproduced evidence (`evidence/C27/raw/baseline.json`):** 15.5 years gives contributions ₹23,25,000 (15.5 yearly deposits) and maturity ₹42,89,102; the table ends at year 16 at ₹45,17,702. The "Optimistic" what-if deposits ₹1,68,000 a year, above the ₹1,50,000 limit. The deposit input accepts any amount.
