# B60 — Deposit-specific FD/CD presentation and visible rate basis

C24. Owner continues direct independent implementation on 2026-10-03 without Gemini. Scopes B50 rows 21 (`fd`) and 78 (`cd`) only; B51, paid/mobile and production gates remain open. Baseline main `04be90d4422e2e3e3c9822dd45f54bda201b27ca`.

**Source convention (inspected, not inferred from route names):** both routes use formula `fd` in `seoCalculators.ts`: `maturity = principal × (1 + rate/100)^years`, interest = maturity − principal. Rate is applied once per year (annual compounding of the entered rate; equivalent to an annual effective yield). A fractional term continues the same annual growth fractionally. CD's input is APY, which already includes compounding, so annual application is correct and must not be converted again (HYSA decision golden: $10,000 at 4.5% APY for 2y → $10,920.25). FD's input is a generic "Annual return / rate" with no stated basis. No payout frequency, tax, fee or penalty arithmetic exists.

**Reproduced evidence (local dev at baseline, 2026-10-03):** both routes say “Enter the deposit amount as a recurring amount”; FD says “Enter the years in years” and “annual return / rate … unless the label says otherwise”; CD says “Enter the apy …” and the sensitivity text says “A higher apy”. Scenario lens/visual call deposit what-ifs “contribution or return assumptions”; “Why it matters” and decision checks require regular contributions and a monthly budget. Example says “Load a sample fd calculator”. No exclusion of taxes, fees or early-withdrawal penalties. Detail schedule rounds term up: an 18-month CD shows final row year 2 at $10,920.25 labelled maturity value while the headline is $10,682.54; intermediate rows are labelled “Maturity value”.

**Sources checked 2026-10-03:** [CFPB: what is a CD](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-certificate-of-deposit-cd-en-917/) (page reviewed 2026-08-28) — term commitment, early withdrawal means paying a penalty; [CFPB Regulation DD Appendix A](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1030/a/) — APY reflects compounding; [RBI FAQs, Interest Rate on Deposits Directions 2025](https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/Upload/English/FAQs/PDFs/FAQIRD01042025.pdf) Q2 reinvestment deposits pay interest with principal at maturity, Q12 premature-withdrawal penalties follow each bank's policy. Sources support the exclusions and definitions only; they do not supply rates, tax entitlement, coverage or compounding frequency for any bank.

## Outcome and scope

`/calculators/fd` and `/calculators/cd` only.

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| R1 | Before the fields, one short deposit basis line states one up-front deposit held to maturity, the actual rate basis (FD: entered yearly rate compounded once a year, interest added and paid with the deposit at maturity; CD: APY already includes compounding and is applied once a year, not converted again) and that taxes/TDS, fees and early-withdrawal (premature) penalties are excluded. |
| R2 | Deposit-specific field labels/helpers: one-time deposit (never recurring); term in years with fractional example; FD rate labelled as an annual interest rate with yearly compounding; CD APY helper. No “label says otherwise”, “years in years” or lowercase “apy”. Input keys, order, limits and defaults unchanged. |
| R3 | Beside the maturity metric, a reconciliation line: deposit + interest earned = maturity value, with term and rate basis, using the existing result metrics. Result object (labels, values, narrative, assumptions) unchanged. |
| R4 | Scenario lens/visual/drawer use neutral deposit labels (“Lower what-if”, “Your deposit”, “Higher what-if”) and describe a joint smaller/shorter/lower-rate versus larger/longer/higher-rate what-if — not an offered rate or forecast. Scenario IDs, input vectors and results unchanged. |
| R5 | Detail schedule: column “Balance” (not maturity value for interim years); a fractional term ends at the exact term with the headline maturity (e.g., row 1.5 = $10,682.54); integer terms unchanged; table, chart-data table and CSV agree with the headline. FD/CD formula only; lumpsum keeps its schedule. |
| R6 | Deposit-specific “why it matters”, decision checks and example copy: compare offers on the same rate basis/term, separate deposit from interest, check taxes/penalties/liquidity. No recurring-contribution or monthly-budget assumption. Example values are labelled illustrative, not current bank rates. |
| R7 | Scope notice (existing `CalculatorScopeNotice`) for both routes with included/excluded/basis and the dated sources above; FD discloses that a bank compounding more often than yearly would mature higher for the same quoted rate and that broken periods may be computed differently. |
| R8 | Shared sensitivity sentence preserves uppercase acronyms (“A higher APY”). Text only; impact arithmetic unchanged. |

**Non-goals:** payout/non-cumulative mode, quarterly or other compounding frequency, recurring deposits, RD copy, fee/tax/penalty arithmetic, automatic or quoted market rates, deposit insurance claims, product recommendations, formula/FIRE/shared-annuity changes, new metrics, new analytics events, D1/schema/auth/dependency changes, production.

**Files:** `seoCalculators.ts` (input labels/helpers only), `calculatorStudios.ts` (labels/descriptions/example/schedule), `calculatorQuality.ts`, `calculatorContent.ts`, `calculatorScope.ts`, `calculatorEngagement.ts` (acronym text), `CalculatorLibrary.tsx` (before-input basis line and maturity reconciliation line), focused tests, `vivid-theme.css` only if a spacing rule is required.
