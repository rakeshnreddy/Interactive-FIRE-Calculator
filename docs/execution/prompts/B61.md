# B61 — RD presentation: deposit timing and interest basis

C25. Owner continues direct independent implementation on 2026-10-03 without Gemini ("finish the work you can do without me unblocking it"). Scopes B50 row 22 (`rd`) only; B51, paid/mobile and production gates remain open. Baseline main `65feeef0bdd6d929c21f71e51ac1b4fc98c239de`.

**Source convention (inspected in `seoCalculators.ts`, not inferred from the route name):** formula `rd` calls `projectRecurringBalance(0, monthly, annualTopUp, rate/12, months)` with `months = max(1, round(years × 12))`. Each month the balance grows by the yearly rate ÷ 12 (monthly compounding of a nominal yearly rate) and then the monthly deposit is added, so each instalment is made at the end of its month and earns from the next month. The optional `annualTopUp` is added after every 12th instalment. Interest = maturity − total deposits. No tax, fee, missed-instalment or premature-withdrawal arithmetic exists.

**Reproduced evidence (local render at baseline, 2026-10-03; `evidence/C25/raw/baseline.json`):** helpers read "Enter the monthly deposit as a recurring amount in the calculator currency", "Enter the years in years", "Enter the annual return / rate as an annual percentage unless the label says otherwise"; the rate is labelled "Annual return / rate"; the top-up is "Additional yearly contribution" in an "Additional contributions" group; the scenario lens says "contribution or return assumptions" with Conservative/Base/Optimistic labels. Nothing states deposit timing, the monthly compounding basis or that tax/TDS, fees and penalties are excluded. The schedule's fractional-term defect is split into B62.

**Sources:** none newly cited. The copy states only the calculator's own convention and what it leaves out; it makes no claim about how any bank compounds or times RD instalments, and says a bank's quote can differ. (The RBI deposits FAQ cited in B60 could not be re-read in this session, so it is not extended to RD.)

## Outcome and scope

`/calculators/rd` only.

| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| R1 | Before the fields, a basis line: equal monthly deposits, each added at the end of its month; interest compounded monthly at the yearly rate ÷ 12 and paid with the deposits at maturity; tax/TDS, fees and missed-instalment or premature-withdrawal penalties not included; a bank that compounds or counts instalments differently will quote a slightly different maturity. |
| R2 | RD-specific labels/helpers: monthly deposit (same amount each month, end of month); "Deposit term" in years, rounded to whole months, with a 1.5 = 18 months example; "Annual interest rate" compounded monthly; the top-up is an optional extra yearly deposit, separate from the monthly instalments, that not every RD accepts, 0 for a standard RD. Optional group says "Extra yearly deposit". Keys, order, limits and defaults unchanged. No "recurring amount … currency", "years in years", "label says otherwise" or "contribution". |
| R3 | Beside the maturity metric: deposits + interest = maturity, with term in months, rate basis and the extra yearly deposit when non-zero, using existing result values. Result object unchanged. |
| R4 | Neutral scenario labels "Lower what-if / Your deposits / Higher what-if" with a joint description (smaller deposit, shorter term, lower rate together), not an offered rate or forecast. Scenario IDs, vectors and results byte-equal to baseline. |
| R5 | RD decision guidance, example insight (illustrative rate, not a bank offer), content blueprint/route angle and a scope notice (included/excluded/basis) for `rd`; no regular-contribution budget language. |

**Non-goals:** start-of-month or quarterly-compounding arithmetic, tax/TDS, fees, penalties, missed instalments, rate lookup, product recommendation, formula/model-version change, analytics, D1/schema/auth/dependency changes, production. Schedule reconciliation belongs to B62.
