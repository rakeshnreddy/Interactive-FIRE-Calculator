- **Homepage copy, icons & flat hero SVG (`R2`, `R3`, `R4`, `R5`, `src/LandingPage.tsx`, `src/lib/landingContent.ts`, `src/HeroFireExample.tsx`):** Dropped trailing periods from single-line headlines (`Clear answers to your money questions`, `Start with a question, leave with a clearer plan`, `Honest math, in your currency`, `Start without an account`), narrowed the hero to one primary CTA (`See when you could retire`) by converting the finder submit to a quiet `.secondary-button` labeled `Search`, standardized all Lucide icons to `16`/`18px` with `strokeWidth={1.5}` and `aria-hidden="true"`, and replaced `<defs><linearGradient id="heroGradient">` and `strokeWidth="2.5"` in `HeroFireExample` with a flat `.hero-svg-area` fill, subtle horizontal mid-rule `.hero-svg-grid`, and `strokeWidth="2"`.
