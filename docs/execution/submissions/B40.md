## What changed, by acceptance item

- **Route inventory and aliases.** 83 registry calculators plus four dedicated ones; `calculatorVariants()` in `src/lib/seoCalculators.ts` identifies calculators sharing a formula and region. Toolkit "View all" lists show one lead row per shared engine with preset chips (EMI/home/car/personal/auto/student loans; snowball vs avalanche with credit-card payoff; SIP family), and detail pages show a "same calculation" switcher. **Every old path still serves its own page** (public smoke, 84 routes).
- **Region.** `RegionFilter` (All / United States / India) synced to `?region=`; region badges on cards and detail headers; tax titles renamed "US Federal Income Tax Estimator", "India Income Tax Calculator: Old vs New Regime", "India Salary Take-home Calculator"; GST and TDS marked India. No targeting language.
- **Copy.** Descriptions are question-led ("Estimate … with the Mortgage Payment Calculator: enter your …") with acronyms preserved (`softLower`). Removed "Model v2", "planning shell", "Need to number" (now "Retirement target"), "display precision" and the precision line. **Copy guard:** `calculatorRegistryAudit.test.ts` fails on internal wording (`SEO`, `traffic`, `ranking`, `phase N`, `Model vN`, `planning shell`, `display precision`, `conversion funnel`) across description, explanation, assumptions and FAQ of all 83 calculators.
- **Orphaned figures.** "Time saved 0" is gone; biweekly and extra-payment calculators report "Months saved" with real values; NPS shows an estimated monthly pension; mortgage shows "Total monthly housing payment".
- **Signed-out navigation.** The Workspace menu (desktop dropdown and mobile group) renders only for signed-in or still-resolving sessions; signed-out visitors see Calculators, FIRE and Sign in. `appParity.test.tsx` asserts the signed-out shell has no workspace navigation.
- **Scenario clarity.** `CalculatorScenarioPanel` lists exactly which inputs the selected scenario changed, with before → after values (`CalculatorLibraryDetail.test.tsx`).
- **Rate ranges.** Registry percent inputs cap at 100; compound interest rejects rates outside −100%..100% with a message; FIRE bounds were set by B36.
- **Gap inventory.** [`docs/calculator-excellence/GAP_MATRIX.md`](../../calculator-excellence/GAP_MATRIX.md): fixed rows with commits and goldens, and open rows (tax statutes, PMI/FHA/VA schedules, Social Security/RMD factors, state stamp duty, chart improvements) that stay visible for later per-route contracts.
