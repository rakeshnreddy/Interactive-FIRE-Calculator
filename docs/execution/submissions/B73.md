## Changed behavior

- **`CBS-01` — Net Worth `toNetWorthResult` headline & `/accounts` save parity (`src/CashflowPlanningCalculator.tsx`):** Reordered `toNetWorthResult` metrics so `Estimated net worth` (`projection.netWorth`) sits at `metrics[0]` ahead of `Total assets`, `Total liabilities`, `Liquid assets`, and `Liquid position`, ensuring saving `/calculators/net-worth` to `/accounts` records `$175,000` (`17500000` cents) rather than gross assets (`$250,000`) and displays `Estimated net worth` in `HistoryPanel`.
- **`CBS-02` — Emergency Fund `incomeStability` persistence (`src/CashflowPlanningCalculator.tsx`):** Added `incomeStabilityToCode` (`0` = `'stable'`, `1` = `'variable'`, `2` = `'uncertain'`) and `incomeStabilityFromSaved` (defaulting legacy snapshots without `incomeStabilityCode` to `'stable'`), persisted `incomeStabilityCode` inside `EmergencyFundCalculator` `saveResult`, and restored `incomeStability` in `loadSaved`.
- **`CBS-04` & `CBS-05` — Life Insurance Needs supporting metrics, category, assumptions, narrative, and 4-bar waterfall (`src/lib/seoCalculators.ts`, `src/lib/calculatorStudios.ts`):** Mapped `formula === 'insurance'` to `category: 'Planning'`; added supporting metrics (`Income replacement need`, `Debts and final expenses`, `Existing savings / coverage`), 2 explicit nominal-multiple assumptions, and an overfunded narrative branch when `need === 0 && grossNeed > 0` in `calculateSeoCalculator`; added `insurance: ['income-replacement', 'debts', 'existing-coverage', 'coverage-gap']` to `costIds` in `selectedModelChart`.
- **`CBS-07` — `CashflowPlanningCalculator` history formatting, select error accessibility, and locale forwarding (`src/CashflowPlanningCalculator.tsx`):** Added `formatSavedMetric` and updated `HistoryPanel` to format metric values (`moneyFormatter`, `percent`, `toLocaleString`) and dates (`toLocaleDateString`) with `resolveMoneyLocale`; extended `PlanningSelectField` with optional `error` (`aria-invalid="true"`, `aria-describedby`, `<small className="field-error" role="alert">`) wired to `incomeStability` and `householdEarners`; forwarded `locale={resolvedLocale}` to `EmergencyRunway` and `BreakdownTable` `percent`.
- **`RINC-07` — FIRE Withdrawal mode optional `Need benchmark` and solved withdrawal projection (`src/App.tsx`):** Added `sanitizePlanForMode(inputPlan, mode)` replacing `NaN` `annualExpense` with `0` at engine boundaries in `'withdrawal-income'` mode while preserving blank `NaN` in React state, wired `effectivePlan` into `calculateFirePlan`, `financialInsights`, and `stressTestCurrentPortfolio`, sanitized `activePlanForDisplay` in `warningNotices` and `comparisonRows` (`activeModeForDisplay` included in `useMemo` deps) plus `nextPlan` in `applySnapshot`, rendered `'No benchmark set'` on `Need coverage`, and switched `Results` table/chart in `'withdrawal-income'` mode to `activeResultForDisplay.portfolioMode.rows` (`'Annual withdrawal projection'`, button `'Annual withdrawal'`).
- **`RINC-12` — `planWarnings` active currency formatting (`src/lib/warnings.ts`, `src/App.tsx`):** Exported `type FireCurrency = 'USD' | 'INR'` in `src/lib/warnings.ts`, added `currency: FireCurrency = 'USD'` to `planWarnings` formatting `requiredGap` via `formatMoney(requiredGap, { currency })`, and passed `fireCurrency` from `src/App.tsx`.
- **Automated tests & C35 hosted smoke (`src/DedicatedPlannersIntegrity.test.tsx`, `scripts/smoke/c35-dedicated-planners.mjs`):** Added 6 red-first tests in `src/DedicatedPlannersIntegrity.test.tsx` (`CBS-01`, `CBS-02`, `CBS-04` & `CBS-05`, `CBS-07`, `RINC-07`, `RINC-12`) and added `scripts/smoke/c35-dedicated-planners.mjs` extending `c31-shell.mjs` with 40 responsive layout checks across 5 routes + 6 interactive DOM & signed-in persistence checks.
