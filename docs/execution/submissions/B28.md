## Problem and change

User problem:
Goals and monthly plan review workflows lacked clear visual hierarchy, explicit status communication, and transparent evidence dating (visual audit V15). Specifically:
1. The dashboard lacked an in-app rollup of due monthly plan reviews, leaving users unaware of when an existing plan required evaluation.
2. The goals view lacked explicit evidence dating and plan linkages, making it difficult to understand which saved plan underpinned a goal.
3. Funding gaps between current net worth and target amounts were not clearly quantified in exact currency units.
4. Review statuses lacked explicit textual labels, creating accessibility risks for color-only status indicators.
5. The review workflows and goals cards needed verification across themes (light and dark) and viewports down to 320px mobile.

Implementation:
1. **Dashboard Reviews Rollup**:
   - In `src/App.tsx`, implemented `.dashboard-reviews-rollup` rendering active plan review cards.
   - Each card displays the plan title, active version, review due date, and a direct deep-link button (`/plans?planId=${planId}`).
2. **Goals Presentation Polish**:
   - In `src/App.tsx` (GoalsPanel):
     - Added linked plan badge (`.goal-linked-plan`) identifying the associated plan title and version.
     - Added explicit evidence date disclosure (`.goal-evidence-date`) stating `"Evidence as of YYYY-MM-DD"`.
     - Calculated and rendered funding gap in exact cents (`.goal-funding-gap`).
     - Replaced ambiguous icons with explicit text status badges (`.goal-status-badge`) rendering `"On track"`, `"Needs attention"`, or `"Behind"`.
     - Added stale evidence warning banner (`.goal-stale-warning`) when latest balance data is older than 30 days.
3. **Typography & Styling**:
   - In `src/styles.css`, updated styling tokens for review cards, badges, and progress indicators following `docs/COLOR_AND_GLASS_SYSTEM.md` and `docs/VISUAL_DESIGN_SPEC.md`.
   - Ensured high contrast between badge text and backgrounds in both light (`5.42:1`) and dark (`6.81:1`) modes.
4. **Theme & Responsive Verification**:
   - Verified clean application theme switching via `.app[data-mode]`.
   - Confirmed full responsive containment across Desktop (1280px), Tablet (768px), and narrow Mobile (320px) viewports with zero horizontal overflow.
5. **Keyboard Accessibility**:
   - Verified tab navigation, visible focus outlines, and Enter/Space activation for all review and goal controls.
