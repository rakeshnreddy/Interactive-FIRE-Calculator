## Problem and change

User problem:
In mobile and desktop loan/mortgage calculators, entering \$200,000 principal at 6.5% interest for 30 years caused the headline "Payoff months" metric to report 361 months, while the Monthly Amortization Schedule table capped at 360 rows. Furthermore, row 360 had a non-zero ending balance ($3.94 \times 10^{-9}) and failed to display the "Final payment" note. In contrast, \$300,000 principal at 6.5% for 30 years happened to produce exactly 0 residual in IEEE 754 arithmetic, masking the divergence on default inputs.

Implementation:
1. Mathematical Analysis and Documentation:
   - Authored `docs/calculators/mortgage-payoff-rounding.md` detailing the standard fixed-payment annuity formula, CFPB Regulation Z (12 CFR Part 1026 Appendix J) computation rules, periodic monthly compounding, and currency tolerance decision.
   - Defined `LOAN_RESIDUAL_TOLERANCE = 0.005` (half a cent) in `src/lib/seoCalculators.ts`. Any residual $\le \$0.005$ is recognized as binary floating-point rounding noise; any residual $>\$0.005$ represents material debt that is never forgiven.
2. Shared Termination & Settlement Rule:
   - In `payoffDebt()` (`src/lib/seoCalculators.ts`):
     - When `currentBalance + monthlyInterest <= scheduledPayment + LOAN_RESIDUAL_TOLERANCE`, the final payment settles the loan (`actualPayment = currentBalance + monthlyInterest`, `newBalance = 0`), terminating at the correct scheduled month.
     - Sub-cent residual adjustments on the final payment prevent extraneous iterations.
   - In `stepAmortizingBalance()` & `amortizationRows()` (`src/lib/calculatorStudios.ts`):
     - When `totalDue <= payment + LOAN_RESIDUAL_TOLERANCE`, the final payment clears remaining balance and accrued interest, setting `endingBalance = 0` and `principalPaid = balance`.
     - Row 360 receives `note: 'Final payment'`, and sum of principal paid across all rows matches initial principal down to the cent.
   - In `debtPayoffSchedule()` (`src/lib/calculatorStudios.ts`):
     - Applied identical tolerance settlement so custom debt payoffs reconcile with headline metrics.
3. Tests and Verification:
   - Added unit test suite `src/lib/mortgageReconciliation.test.ts` covering:
     - \$200,000 / 6.5% / 30y reconciliation (360 months, 360 rows, ending balance \$0, note "Final payment").
     - \$300,000 / 6.5% / 30y baseline preservation.
     - 0% interest rate loan (120 months for 10y, no division by zero).
     - Extra monthly principal with final payment smaller than regular payment.
     - 15-year term (180 months, verifying 360 is never hardcoded).
     - Extra annual payment across year boundaries.
     - Material debt preservation (balance \$1,000 at 0% with \$300 payment requires 4 months, not 3).
   - Created browser verification script `docs/execution/evidence/B08/verify_b08.cjs` testing mobile 390px in Light and Dark modes for both \$200k and \$300k, confirming 0 console errors and 0 page exceptions.
