## Problem and change

User problem:
In mobile and desktop loan/mortgage calculators, entering \$200,000 principal at 6.5% interest for 30 years caused the headline "Payoff months" metric to report 361 months, while the Monthly Amortization Schedule table capped at 360 rows. Furthermore, row 360 had a non-zero ending balance ($3.94 \times 10^{-9}) and failed to display the "Final payment" note. In contrast, \$300,000 principal at 6.5% for 30 years happened to produce exactly 0 residual in IEEE 754 arithmetic, masking the divergence on default inputs. In C04 initial review (R2), opening guard and schedule loops erased positive sub-cent principal (e.g. 0.005) before payments began, and boundary cases lacked exact coverage.

Implementation:
1. Mathematical Analysis and Documentation:
   - Authored and clarified `docs/calculators/mortgage-payoff-rounding.md` detailing the standard fixed-payment annuity formula, CFPB Regulation Z computation rules, periodic monthly compounding, and currency tolerance decision.
   - Corrected rounding explanation: clarified that under half-up rounding conventions, exactly 0.005 rounds to 0.01 (not 0), and documented FinPath's inclusive settlement policy ($currentBalance + interest \le scheduledPayment + 0.005$) as an explicit financial modeling rule for final payment closure.
   - Clarified rate conversion: frontend annual rate 6.5% converts to 0.065, then monthly 0.065/12; decimal 0.065 is not divided by 100 again.
   - Defined `LOAN_RESIDUAL_TOLERANCE = 0.005` (half a cent) in `src/lib/seoCalculators.ts`.
2. Opening Principal Preservation & Settlement Rule:
   - In `payoffDebt()` (`src/lib/seoCalculators.ts`):
     - Opening guard changed to `balance <= 0`. Positive opening principal (e.g. 0.005) is preserved and processed through actual payment settlement.
     - When `currentBalance + monthlyInterest <= scheduledPayment + LOAN_RESIDUAL_TOLERANCE`, the final payment settles the loan (`actualPayment = currentBalance + monthlyInterest`, `newBalance = 0`), terminating at the correct scheduled month.
   - In `clampInput()`, `stepAmortizingBalance()`, `amortizationRows()`, and `debtPayoffSchedule()` (`src/lib/calculatorStudios.ts`):
     - Changed loop conditions and opening guards to `balance <= 0` / `balance > 0`, preserving precision and preventing premature termination.
     - Row 360 receives `note: 'Final payment'`, and sum of principal paid across all rows matches initial principal down to the cent.
3. Tests and Verification:
   - Added unit test suite `src/lib/mortgageReconciliation.test.ts` (9/9 tests passing) covering:
     - Positive opening balance 0.005 with payment 0.001 (5 months, total paid 0.005).
     - Boundary cases 1000.004 (1 month, paid 1000.004), 1000.005 (1 month, paid 1000.005), 1000.006 (2 months, paid 1000.006).
     - \$200,000 / 6.5% / 30y reconciliation (360 months, 360 rows, ending balance \$0, note "Final payment").
     - \$300,000 / 6.5% / 30y baseline preservation.
     - 0% interest rate loan (120 months for 10y, no division by zero).
     - Extra monthly principal with final payment smaller than regular payment.
     - 15-year term (180 months, verifying 360 is never hardcoded).
     - Extra annual payment across year boundaries.
     - Material debt preservation (balance \$1,000 at 0% with \$300 payment requires 4 months, not 3).
   - Created browser verification script `docs/execution/evidence/B08/verify_b08.cjs` and combined verifier `docs/execution/evidence/C04/verify_hosted_c04.cjs`.
