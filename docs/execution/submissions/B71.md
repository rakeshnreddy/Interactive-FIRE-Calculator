## Changed behavior

- **Shared 600-month debt horizon (`src/lib/debtProjection.ts`):** `debt-payoff`, `credit-card-payoff`, `credit-card-minimum-payment`, `student-loan-payoff`, `balance-transfer`, and `debt-snowball-avalanche` now share one pure simulation module (`DEBT_HORIZON_MONTHS = 600`, `DEBT_RESIDUAL_TOLERANCE = 0.005`). Engine and schedule no longer diverge between a 600-month headline cap and a 360-month table cap.
- **Unpaid / stalled debt payoff truth (`R1`, `R2`):** When a balance remains positive after 600 months (`DEBT_HORIZON_MONTHS`), `debt-payoff` routes report `'Balance after 50 years'` (`primaryUnit: 'currency'`) instead of a false `'50.0 years'` payoff duration, include `'Balance after 50 years'` and `'Months modeled': 600` in metrics, and emit a Year 50 schedule row whose ending balance matches the headline.
- **Balance transfer and debt strategy reconciliation (`R3`, `R4`):** `balance-transfer` compares the transfer path and existing-card path on the same horizon, suppresses false interest-saved headlines when either path remains unpaid at 600 months (`'Transfer balance after 50 years'`), and reconciles the Year 1–50 schedule with both paths. `debt-snowball-avalanche` switches from `'Interest saved vs snowball'` to `'Avalanche balance after 50 years'` when either strategy fails to clear within 600 months and reconciles the final schedule row with both strategies' ending balances.
- **Mortgage recast cash outlay & interest saved (`R5`):** `mortgage-recast` now reports `Total recast cash outlay` (`lumpSum + recastFee`), caps `lumpSum` at the remaining balance before recast, models `Total interest with recast` and `Interest saved vs original remaining term` alongside `Monthly payment drop`, and reconciles its comparison schedule rows (`Original remaining loan`, `After recast`, `Lump sum without recast fee`).
- **Immediate balloon due (`R6`):** `balloon-loan` with `balloonYears <= 0` reports the full loan amount due immediately at month 0 (`Monthly payment`: `0`, `Total interest`: `0`) and emits a single `Year 0 (Immediate)` schedule row matching the full balance.
- **Conventional PMI 80% LTV rule & US region tags (`R7`):** `fha-vs-conventional` charges `0` initial conventional monthly PMI when initial LTV is `80%` or below (`downPaymentPercent >= 20`) and updates `fhaConventionalComparisonSchedule` accordingly. `pmi` charges `0` monthly PMI when `loanAmount <= homeValue * 0.80`, titles its schedule `'PMI cost breakdown'`, and cites CFPB guidance distinguishing `80%` borrower-requested cancellation from `78%` automatic termination. `pmi` and `heloc` are explicitly tagged `region: 'US'`.
- **Rent-vs-buy schedule fallback preservation (`R8`):** `buildCalculatorDetailSchedule` preserves the `inputs.loanYears ?? inputs.years ?? 30` fallback and `rentBuySchedule` remains covered by regression tests.
