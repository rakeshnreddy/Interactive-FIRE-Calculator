## Changed behavior

- **Word-bounded scenario input classification & fixed statutory/target inputs (`R1`):** Replaced unanchored substring matching in `adjustInput` (`src/lib/calculatorStudios.ts`) with word-bounded (`\b`) and key-aware rules so `"current"` (`currentSavings` on `retirement`, `principal` "Current 401(k)" on `401k`, `current` "Current savings" on `sip-goal` and `savings-goal`) is never misclassified as `"rent"`, and `income` / `principal` / `Pre-tax` are never misclassified as expenses or taxes while `preTaxDeductions` on `paycheck` perturbs consistently with `postTaxDeductions`. Held statutory/target parameters fixed (`periods` on `paycheck`, `metroPercent` on `hra-exemption`, `annuityPercent` on `nps`, `annualIncome` on `retirement`, `target` on `sip-goal`/`savings-goal`, `principal` on `inflation`), and rounded perturbed inputs with `Number(rawAdjusted.toFixed(6))` to eliminate floating-point tails (`112000.00000000001`).
- **Directionally truthful scenarios across Debt, Loan, Tax, Retirement, and Insurance (`R2`):** Held entered debt balances fixed on `debt-payoff`, `balance-transfer`, and `debt-snowball-avalanche` (while increasing balances in Conservative and decreasing in Optimistic on remaining `Debt Payoff Studio` routes) so `extra-mortgage-payment` and `mortgage-payoff` Conservative scenarios pay off within 50 years (`29.5 years`) and comparative savings routes (`balance-transfer`, `debt-snowball-avalanche`, `mortgage-refinance`, `home-loan-balance-transfer-india`, `mortgage-points`, `home-loan-prepayment`, `home-loan-foreclosure`) keep `Conservative <= Base <= Optimistic`. Added explicit scenario handlers for `mortgage-recast`, `closing-costs`, `balloon-loan`, `roth-traditional`, `hra`, `insurance`, `social-security`, and `rmd` with truthful scenario descriptions.
- **Return Analysis scenarios & signed loss perturbation (`R3`):** Added `xirr` to `neutralCaseFormulas` (`'Case A'`, `'Your inputs'`, `'Case B'` labels, sensitivity check description, and `'neutral'` chart tones), protected short holding periods (`years <= 1`) on `investment-return`, `cagr`, and `xirr` with fractional steps (`>= 0.25` yrs), and scaled signed `gain` on `roi` by `value + direction * (Math.abs(value) > 0 ? Math.abs(value) * 0.08 : 100)` while holding `cost` fixed when `gain < 0`.
- **Complete scenario card changed-input disclosure (`R4`):** Removed `.slice(0, 3)` in `changedInputsLabel` (`src/CalculatorLibrary.tsx`) so all perturbed inputs are disclosed on scenario cards.
- **Bidirectional sensitivity drivers & supporting-metric fallback (`R5`):** Updated `directionFrom` (`src/lib/calculatorEngagement.ts`) to derive `'raises'`/`'lowers'` from the lower-bound delta when `higherOutput === baseOutput` at upper input caps (`ppf` `annual = 150000`, `mortgage-affordability` `maxDti = 28%`, `home-loan-foreclosure` `prepayment = 3500000`). Added whole-year step sizing on `ppf` `years`, 10% proportional step sizing on `<5%` positive `percent` inputs, realistic step sizing on zero-default currency inputs, same-unit supporting-metric fallback (`isPrimaryDriver`, `targetValueType`) with primary-driver display priority in `.calculator-driver-list`, and accurate loss-cost wording on `roi`.
- **Pinned vector preservation (`R6`):** Preserved all pinned scenario input/output vectors on `fd`, `cd`, `rd`, `lumpsum-mutual-fund`, `sip`, `step-up-sip`, `ppf`, `investment-return`, and `cagr`, plus `Conservative` / `Base` / `Optimistic` labels on `mortgage`.
