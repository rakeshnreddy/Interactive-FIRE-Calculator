# B66 — Truthful generated input helpers

Astra direct implementation, 2026-10-04; no Gemini. Contract [B66](../prompts/B66.md), registered in C28 at `f0b67fe`. Accepted in [C28](../reviews/C28.md).

**What changed (`defaultInputHelper` in `seoCalculators.ts` only).** Percent: taxes, TDS, GST, duties, registration, fees, closing costs, down payment, caps, income ratios, PF/payroll deductions and withholding read "Enter as a percentage, for example 18 for 18%."; other rates read "Yearly rate as a percentage, for example 7.5 for 7.5% a year." Currency: "Amount each month/year in the calculator currency." only when the label says monthly, annual, yearly or CTC; otherwise the neutral "Enter the X in the calculator currency." Time: "per year" counts are checked first; the input's unit suffix decides years ("Number of years; 1.5 means 18 months." / "In years; 1.5 means 18 months.") or months ("Number of months, for example 6 or 18."). Explicit helpers are untouched.

**Evidence.** Survey of all 359 inputs on 82 routes: 196 helpers changed; none keep "unless the label says otherwise", "recurring amount" or "… in years." A first draft gave "Fixed period", "Promo period" and "Quoted lease period" the pay-periods text and "Years delayed in years"; the full before/after listing caught both and the unit-suffix rule replaced them before commit. An existing guard (helpers longer than 20 characters) failed on "In months." and was satisfied with the fuller months text rather than relaxed.

**Tests.** `src/GeneratedInputHelpers.test.ts`: 4 red first, the explicit-helper/result guard passing. Final runner **104 files / 2,760 tests**; CI 37186645870.
