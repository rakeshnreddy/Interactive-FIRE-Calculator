# B62 — Exact-term schedules for lump sum and monthly recurring calculators

Astra direct implementation, 2026-10-03, same commit and evidence as [B61](B61.md). Contract [B62](../prompts/B62.md). Accepted in [C25](../reviews/C25.md).

**What changed (`calculatorStudios.ts` only).** `singleDepositGrowthSchedule` (lump sum) now steps whole years then the exact remaining part year, returns no table for a zero term, and labels columns "Growth this period / Total growth / Value" with a part-year note. `recurringGrowthSchedule` runs `termMonths(years)` — the engine's own month count — instead of `ceil(years) × 12`; a final part-year row shows the exact term (e.g. 1.5) and "Part year: 6 months of deposits and growth." HYSA's APY path is unchanged. This corrects compound interest, SIP, step-up SIP, RD and NPS schedules.

**Evidence.** Baseline at 1.5 years ([`baseline.json`](../evidence/C25/manifest.json)): lump sum ₹5,61,184.46 headline versus ₹5,83,200 final row; RD/SIP ₹1,90,571.91 versus ₹2,59,331.90; step-up SIP, compound and NPS likewise. After: every listed calculator's last row equals its headline (tests, 1e-6), independent Decimal oracles 561184.4616523162, 190571.9050506165 and 195775.2681621231 (extra deposit at month 12) match. Whole-year rows are value-identical to the captured baseline. Hosted CSVs for an 18-month RD with an extra deposit (₹1,95,775) and a 1.5-year lump sum (₹5,61,184) equal their headlines.

**Not changed.** Engines, results, scenario vectors, other schedule builders (residual below). No model version, schema or dependency change.

**Residual.** Other builders still use whole-year rounding and were not audited for fractional terms: EPF, PPF, savings goal, inflation, investment return, XIRR approximation, Roth/traditional, rent-vs-buy, interest-only, flat-rate comparison and gratuity. Several have integer-only inputs in practice; each needs its own check before any change.
