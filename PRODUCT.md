# FinPath Product Context

## Register

Product

## Users

FinPath serves people in the United States and India who want to understand a financial decision before committing to it, then keep that decision connected to their real accounts, goals, transactions, and plans. Public calculator users may be anonymous. Signed-in users expect private, durable, identity-scoped financial records.

## Product Purpose

FinPath turns one-off financial calculations into understandable decisions and revisitable plans. It should help someone answer a focused question quickly, explain what moved the answer, compare realistic alternatives, and continue into tracking without forcing an account before the calculation is useful.

## Brand Personality

- Clear: plain language, visible assumptions, and no hidden math.
- Composed: calm enough for consequential decisions and dense enough for repeated use.
- Practical: every surface should help someone calculate, compare, save, or track.
- Quietly optimistic: progress is shown without gamification or false certainty.

## Anti-References

- An endless directory of nearly identical calculator cards.
- Generic fintech composition or decorative glass on every surface. The owner-approved light/dark material direction in docs/COLOR_AND_GLASS_SYSTEM.md permits purposeful gradients and bounded glass.
- Marketing copy that exposes traffic, acquisition, or search strategy.
- Oversized promotional layouts inside operational tools.
- Financial estimates presented as promises or professional advice.
- Playful finance metaphors, novelty controls, or decorative motion.

## Design Principles

1. Start with the decision. Organize tools by the question a person is trying to answer, while preserving exact calculator names and routes.
2. Explain the number. Inputs, outputs, units, assumptions, scenarios, and detailed schedules should be understandable in place.
3. One system, many entry points. Related calculators share a toolkit and interaction model without becoming duplicate pages.
4. Earn every layer. Use glass, color, cards, and disclosure only when they clarify hierarchy or interaction.
5. Preserve trust. Keep calculators public, account data private, estimates clearly labeled, and conversion prompts secondary to the result.

## Accessibility And Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, 200% zoom, reduced motion, reduced transparency, and color-independent status cues. Use locale-aware number and currency formatting and avoid assuming that US and India terminology are interchangeable.
